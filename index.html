Research interests

    Cloud runtimes, fast startup, and adaptive resource management.

  • Virtualization & operating systems

    Lightweight isolation, scheduling, and multi-tenant infrastructure.

  • Computer networks

    Network-aware system design and efficient cloud data paths.
